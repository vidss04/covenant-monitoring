Section A0. Preamble.
FIRST AMENDMENT TO CREDIT AGREEMENT dated as of May 20, 2025 among Apex Industrial Holdings,
Inc., the Lenders party hereto and Northgate Capital Partners LLC, as Administrative Agent.
SYNTHETIC DOCUMENT.

Section A2(a). Amendment to Section 7.11(a).
Section 7.11(a) of the Credit Agreement is hereby amended by replacing the reference to "4.50
to 1.00" with "4.25 to 1.00".

Section A3. Conditions to Effectiveness.
This Amendment shall become effective as of June 30, 2025 (the "Amendment Effective Date") and
the amendments in Section 2 shall apply to each Test Period ending on or after the Amendment
Effective Date.

Section A4. Fees.
The Borrower shall pay to each consenting Lender an amendment fee equal to 0.25% of such
Lender's Commitments. Except as expressly amended hereby, the Credit Agreement remains in full
force.
