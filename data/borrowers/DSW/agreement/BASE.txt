Section 0. Preamble.
CREDIT AGREEMENT dated as of January 22, 2024 among Delta Software Group, Inc., as the
Borrower, the Lenders party hereto from time to time, and Northgate Capital Partners LLC, as
Administrative Agent. THIS DOCUMENT IS SYNTHETIC AND WAS CREATED FOR AN ACADEMIC PROJECT.

Section 1.01(a). Defined Term: EBITDA before Adjustments.
"EBITDA before Adjustments" means, for any Test Period, consolidated operating income of the
Borrower and its Restricted Subsidiaries for such Test Period plus depreciation and
amortization expense, in each case determined in accordance with GAAP.

Section 1.01. Defined Term: Consolidated EBITDA.
"Consolidated EBITDA" means, for any Test Period, EBITDA before Adjustments for such Test
Period plus, without duplication and to the extent deducted in computing such amount: (a) Non-
Recurring Charges; (b) Pro Forma Cost Savings; (c) Stock-Based Compensation. No other item
shall be added back to Consolidated EBITDA except as expressly set forth in this definition.

Section 1.01(f). Defined Term: Consolidated EBITDA (limitation).
Provided that the aggregate amount added back pursuant to clause (a) and clause (b) for any
Test Period shall not exceed 12.5% of EBITDA before Adjustments for such Test Period
(calculated before giving effect to any such add-back).

Section 1.01(g). Defined Term: Total Debt.
"Total Debt" means the aggregate principal amount of all funded Indebtedness of the Borrower
and its Restricted Subsidiaries, including Revolving Loans, Term Loans and any notes.

Section 1.01(h). Defined Term: Net Debt.
"Net Debt" means Total Debt minus all Unrestricted Cash.

Section 1.01(i). Defined Term: Total Net Leverage Ratio.
"Total Net Leverage Ratio" means, as of the last day of any Test Period, the ratio of (a) Net
Debt as of such day to (b) Consolidated EBITDA for such Test Period.

Section 1.01(j). Defined Term: Interest Coverage Ratio.
"Interest Coverage Ratio" means the ratio of (a) Consolidated EBITDA to (b) Consolidated
Interest Expense, in each case for such Test Period.

Section 1.01(k). Defined Term: Test Period.
"Test Period" means, as of any date, the period of four consecutive fiscal quarters then most
recently ended.

Section 2.01. Commitments.
Subject to the terms and conditions set forth herein, each Lender agrees to make Term Loans to
the Borrower on the Closing Date in an aggregate principal amount not exceeding its Term
Commitment, and Revolving Loans from time to time during the Availability Period. Amounts
repaid in respect of Term Loans may not be reborrowed.

Section 2.08. Interest.
The Loans comprising each Term SOFR Borrowing shall bear interest at Adjusted Term SOFR for the
Interest Period in effect plus the Applicable Rate. The Applicable Rate shall be determined by
reference to the leverage-based pricing grid set forth in Schedule 2.08, with step-downs of 25
basis points at each level below 4.00 to 1.00, 3.50 to 1.00 and 3.00 to 1.00.

Section 2.11. Mandatory Prepayments.
Within five Business Days after financial statements are delivered for any fiscal year, the
Borrower shall prepay Term Loans in an amount equal to 50% of Excess Cash Flow, reduced to 25%
if the leverage ratio as of the last day of such fiscal year is less than 3.00 to 1.00 and to
0% if less than 2.50 to 1.00.

Section 2.20. Incremental Facilities.
The Borrower may request Incremental Term Loans in an aggregate amount not to exceed the
greater of $50,000,000 and 100% of Consolidated EBITDA, plus an unlimited amount so long as, on
a pro forma basis, the leverage ratio would not exceed 4.75 to 1.00. This incurrence test is
not a maintenance covenant.

Section 5.01. Financial Statements.
The Borrower shall deliver to the Administrative Agent (a) within 90 days after the end of each
fiscal year, audited consolidated financial statements, and (b) within 45 days after the end of
each of the first three fiscal quarters, unaudited consolidated financial statements, in each
case certified by a Financial Officer as presenting fairly in all material respects the
financial condition of the Borrower.

Section 5.02. Compliance Certificate.
Concurrently with any delivery of financial statements under Section 5.01, the Borrower shall
deliver a certificate of a Financial Officer substantially in the form of Exhibit D (a)
certifying that no Default has occurred, and (b) setting forth reasonably detailed calculations
demonstrating compliance with the financial covenants, including a reconciliation of each add-
back to Consolidated EBITDA.

Section 5.09. Information Regarding Collateral.
The Borrower shall furnish to the Administrative Agent prompt written notice of any change in
its legal name, jurisdiction of organization or organizational identification number.

Section 6.10(a). Financial Covenant: Total Net Leverage Ratio.
The Borrower shall not permit the Total Net Leverage Ratio as of the last day of any Test
Period to exceed the ratio set forth below opposite such Test Period: for any Test Period
ending on or before June 30, 2025, 6.00 to 1.00; for any Test Period ending after June 30, 2025
and on or before December 31, 2025, 5.75 to 1.00; for any Test Period ending after December 31,
2025, 5.50 to 1.00.

Section 6.10(b). Financial Covenant: Interest Coverage Ratio.
The Borrower shall not permit the Interest Coverage Ratio for any Test Period to be less than
2.00 to 1.00.

Section 8.01. Events of Default.
Each of the following shall constitute an Event of Default: (a) failure to pay principal when
due; (b) failure to pay interest within three Business Days; (c) failure to observe any
financial covenant; (d) failure to deliver the Compliance Certificate within five Business Days
after it is due.

Section 8.04. Right to Cure.
Notwithstanding Section 8.01, in the event the Borrower fails to comply with a financial
covenant, the Borrower may within 15 Business Days after the Compliance Certificate is due
receive a Specified Equity Contribution, which shall increase Consolidated EBITDA solely for
purposes of determining compliance; provided that no more than two such cures may be made in
any four consecutive fiscal quarters and no more than five during the term of this Agreement.
