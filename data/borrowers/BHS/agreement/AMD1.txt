Section A0. Preamble.
AMENDMENT NO. 1 TO CREDIT AGREEMENT dated as of March 14, 2025 among Brightwater Health
Services, LLC, the Lenders party hereto and Northgate Capital Partners LLC, as Administrative
Agent. SYNTHETIC DOCUMENT.

Section A2(a). Amendment to definition of Consolidated EBITDA.
The definition of "Consolidated EBITDA" in Section 1.01 is hereby amended by adding a new
clause permitting the add-back of Run-Rate Synergies, which shall be subject to the same
limitation as Pro Forma Cost Savings.

Section A2(b). Amendment to Section 1.01(e).
Section 1.01(e) of the Credit Agreement is hereby amended and restated so that the aggregate
amount of Pro Forma Cost Savings and Run-Rate Synergies added back for any Test Period shall
not exceed 20% of EBITDA before Adjustments for such Test Period.

Section A3. Conditions to Effectiveness.
This Amendment shall become effective as of March 31, 2025 (the "Amendment Effective Date") and
the amendments in Section 2 shall apply to each Test Period ending on or after the Amendment
Effective Date.

Section A4. Fees.
The Borrower shall pay to each consenting Lender an amendment fee equal to 0.25% of such
Lender's Commitments. Except as expressly amended hereby, the Credit Agreement remains in full
force.
