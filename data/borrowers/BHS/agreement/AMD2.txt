Section A0. Preamble.
AMENDMENT NO. 2 TO CREDIT AGREEMENT dated as of February 27, 2026 among Brightwater Health
Services, LLC, the Lenders party hereto and Northgate Capital Partners LLC, as Administrative
Agent. SYNTHETIC DOCUMENT.

Section A2(a). Amendment to Section 6.12(a).
Section 6.12(a) of the Credit Agreement is hereby amended by replacing the reference to "5.00
to 1.00" with "5.25 to 1.00".

Section A3. Conditions to Effectiveness.
This Amendment shall become effective as of March 31, 2026 (the "Amendment Effective Date") and
the amendments in Section 2 shall apply to each Test Period ending on or after the Amendment
Effective Date.

Section A4. Fees.
The Borrower shall pay to each consenting Lender an amendment fee equal to 0.25% of such
Lender's Commitments. Except as expressly amended hereby, the Credit Agreement remains in full
force.
