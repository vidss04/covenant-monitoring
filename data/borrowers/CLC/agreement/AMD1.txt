Section A0. Preamble.
FIRST AMENDMENT TO CREDIT AGREEMENT dated as of May 10, 2026 among Cedar Logistics Corp., the
Lenders party hereto and Northgate Capital Partners LLC, as Administrative Agent. SYNTHETIC
DOCUMENT.

Section A2(a). Amendment to Section 7.1(a).
Section 7.1(a) of the Credit Agreement is hereby amended by replacing the reference to "3.75 to
1.00" with "3.50 to 1.00".

Section A3. Conditions to Effectiveness.
This Amendment shall become effective on the first day of the first fiscal quarter commencing
after the consummation of the Specified Acquisition (the "Amendment Effective Date"), and the
amendments in Section 2 shall apply to each Test Period ending on or after the Amendment
Effective Date.

Section A4. Fees.
The Borrower shall pay to each consenting Lender an amendment fee equal to 0.25% of such
Lender's Commitments. Except as expressly amended hereby, the Credit Agreement remains in full
force.
